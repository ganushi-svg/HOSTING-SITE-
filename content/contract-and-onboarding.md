# contract-and-onboarding

## Onboarding steps (4 Nov 2026)
Ganushi asked for the general steps to be set out from the known terms. Each step restates facts below or in content/pricing.md and content/services.md.
1. Request a quote: send models and quantity through the form or WhatsApp.
2. Agree the terms: your rate by fleet size and the hosting contract, 12 months minimum.
3. Setup fee and deposit: USD 100 per kW setup; deposit of one month of hosting; USD or AED, by USDT, AED (bank transfer or cash) or BTC.
4. Deliver your machines: delivery charged by distance, as set out in the hosting contract.
5. Racked and running: installed in an air-cooled hall or a hydro container and tracked on the fleet platform.
6. Monthly invoice and report: invoice at the start of each month in your client group, with running days, energy and charge per machine.

## Known terms
- Minimum contract term: 12 months (Ganushi KC, 4 Nov 2026).
- Payment currency: USD or AED (Ganushi KC, 4 Nov 2026).
- Payment methods: USDT; AED by bank transfer or cash; BTC (Ganushi KC, 4 Nov 2026).
- Who can host: any individual or company; any number of machines (Ganushi KC, 4 Nov 2026).
- Payment terms: a deposit of one month of hosting; the hosting invoice is shared at the start of every month in each client's group (Ganushi KC, 4 Nov 2026).
- Machine delivery and return: charged by distance, and handled as set out in the hosting contract (Ganushi KC, 4 Nov 2026).
