# brand

## Website palette: "Segments purple" (Ganushi KC, 4 Nov 2026)
Institutional, high-conversion look in the style of enterprise cloud providers. Replaces the blue-and-gold "Midnight Glow" palette on the website.
- Canvas background: deep midnight void #0B0711
- Section and card surface: #161022, 1px border #2D2342, rounded-2xl (16 px) corners, generous padding
- Headings: ice-white #F8FAFC with slight tight tracking; secondary text and units (/ kWh): #94A3B8
- Primary CTA ("Request a quote", "Get a quote"): Deep Violet #7C3AED, ice-white text, soft purple glow; hover #6D28D9
- Active states, key numbers (45 MW, 95%, 3,000+), highlighted cards and trust badges: Electric Orchid #C084FC
- Live calculator outputs and calculator highlights only: Cyan #22D3EE
- The logo itself keeps its own blue and gold.

## Brand banners (added 4 Nov 2026)
Two wide banners supplied by Ganushi KC: "Segments Cloud" (blue and gold mark on navy fading to sky, public/brand/banner-cloud.webp, shown at the top of Services) and "Segments Repair" (line-art mark with a wrench, public/brand/banner-repair.webp). The Repair banner was taken down the same day at Ganushi's request; its right-hand line-art is instead extracted as a transparent tinted print (public/brand/repair-lineart.webp) and shown faintly behind the six-step repair panel. Re-encoded to WebP at 1600 px with metadata stripped.
