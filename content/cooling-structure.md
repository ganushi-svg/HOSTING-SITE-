# Cooling structures (layer diagrams in the Sites tabs)

Added 4 Nov 2026 at Ganushi KC's request: an interactive section drawing of the air-cooled hall and of the hydro container, so clients see what each layer does.

## Air-cooled hall
Source: Drawing SC-CH-01 "Evaporative Cooling Hall - Plan View & Section A-A", Segments Cloud Electronics LLC, 29 Oct 2026 (AutoCAD PDF uploaded by Ganushi KC, 4 Nov 2026).
Airflow noted on the drawing: outside > hood > panel filters > wet pad > dry pad > cold aisle > miners > hot aisle > exhaust fans.
Measured on site (mm): filter to wet pad 940; wet pad to dry pad 880; dry pad to rack board 2,000 (1,500 clear); rack board 500; hot aisle 1,500. Section width 5,870.
Marked "assumed, verify on site" and therefore NOT published: hall length 12,000, ceiling 3,000, pad thickness 150, curb 600, fan size 1,380, 4 fans.
Layers published, in airflow order: intake hood with bird mesh; panel filters (mesh GI pre-filter and disposable filters, schedules from content/quality.md); filter corridor; wet evaporative pad with water header; service corridor; dry pad; cold aisle; miner racks through the rack board; hot aisle; exhaust fans.
The one-line role of each layer is written from the drawing and standard evaporative-hall practice. Dry pad wording (droplet catch and airflow straightening) confirmed by Ganushi KC, 4 Nov 2026.

## Hydro container
Sources: Drive › "HFSG1200 AlKhazna Commissioning Summer Readiness Plan.docx" (Rev 0, 19 Aug 2026) and the container user manual it cites; content/quality.md (weekly inspection, coolant standards).
Published structure, following the coolant: hydro miners on racks, each on quick-connect supply and return hoses with a mini valve; rack manifolds; sealed internal loop with circulation pump, automatic air vents and a make-up tank with sight glass; plate heat exchanger separating the sealed loop from the tower water; leak-detection strips, temperature, pressure and flow transmitters and the PLC/HMI; external pipework with butterfly valves to the tower; dry/wet hybrid cooling tower (coil for dry mode, spray nozzles, fill and drift eliminator for wet mode, fans); tower basin with float valve, suction strainer, side-stream filter and conductivity-controlled blowdown; treated water supply with buffer tank.
Figures published: loop pressure held at or above 0.05 MPa (auto mode), flow at or above 85 m³/h, container tank at or above 2/3 on the sight glass, coolant pH 7 to 9 (all already in content/quality.md); tower rated for 35 °C supply and 45 °C return.
Not published (internal): site names, slot counts, hashrate, water volumes, costs, derating history, valve numbers.
