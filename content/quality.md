## Hydro containers: weekly cleaning and inspection
Source: Drive › Weekly Cleaning SOP (hydro container + closed-circuit cooling tower), Rev C, September 2026.
- A fixed weekly routine of 9 steps, about 110 minutes, early morning.
- Steps: walk-around; record temperatures, pressures and flow; sump check; spray-pump filter clean; louvres and grilles; nozzles and drift eliminator; inside the container (hoses, leak-detection belt, pumps); coolant tank level; coil check and close-out.
- Targets on the site SOP: coolant flow at or above 85 m³/h, return pressure above 0.05 MPa, make-up tank at two-thirds or more.
- Two-water rule: nothing but the approved coolant ever enters the sealed coolant loop.
- Every visit ends with a signed, photographed log sheet; abnormal findings are escalated.
- Schedule: daily checks, the weekly routine, and 6-monthly and yearly service (coolant pH every 6 months, a coolant lab test yearly before summer, a pipe leak survey every 6 months).

## Hydro coolant quality
Source: Drive › Hydro_Miner_Coolant_Quality_SOP_Checklist.
- Distilled or deionised water only, never tap water.
- Weekly visual check (no rust, sediment or algae) and level check, topped up with the same coolant only.
- Conductivity, pH and refractometer tests: pH 7.0 to 9.0; under 5 µS/cm for deionised water; 30 to 50% for glycol mixes.
- Quarterly: inspect tubing and fittings for corrosion, leaks and deposits; flush if coolant is over 6 months old or contaminated.
- Every check logged with date, values and top-ups. Only approved additives.
